Child safety standards

Six Vibes is for adults only (18+). We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind.

How we keep minors off Six Vibes

  • Age check before anything else. Every new account must pass an age check (selfie with liveness and face-age estimation by our provider Didit, plus an ID check whenever the estimate is close to 18) before it can browse, message, appear to others or upload anything.
  • Every photo is checked before it goes live, including for suspected minors. When in doubt, a photo is rejected.
  • Text only. Chats can't contain images, files or links.

Reporting

Every profile and every message has a Report button with a "Suspected minor" reason. Those reports are acted on immediately: the reported account is suspended automatically, before any human review, and then investigated.

You can also report concerns to info@sixvibes.org — our child safety point of contact.

What we do

  • Accounts involved in CSAE are permanently removed.
  • Any CSAM we become aware of is preserved as required and reported to the competent authorities — in the Netherlands the police and the Expertisebureau Online Kindermisbruik (EOKM, Meldpunt Kinderporno) — and to other authorities where the law requires.
  • We cooperate with law-enforcement requests made under applicable law.

We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, including EU and Dutch law.
