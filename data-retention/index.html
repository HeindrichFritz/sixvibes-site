Six Vibes — Data Retention Schedule

Draft — this document is under legal review and may change before launch.

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Controller: [OPERATOR NAME], info@sixvibes.org

General rule: we keep your data while your account exists, and delete it when you delete your account (Profile → Settings → Delete account), except where a longer period is listed below for safety or legal reasons. Backups held by our hosting provider roll off within their backup window.

# Data category Purpose Retention period Deletion method
1 Email address, sign-in records Account and sign-in Until account deletion Deleted with the account (auth record deleted, cascades to all linked data)
2 Sign-in session token (on your phone) Staying signed in Until you sign out or delete the app Removed from device storage on sign-out
3 Terms acceptance timestamp Proof of agreement Until account deletion Deleted with the account
4 Age-verification result (yes/no + time) 18+ enforcement Until account deletion Deleted with the account
5 Age-verification selfie Age estimation Not stored by us. Held by Didit only for the check: [PROVIDER RETENTION] Never written to our storage; provider deletion per DPA
6 Display name, bio, vibes Profile Until you change it or delete your account Overwritten on edit; deleted with the account
7 Gender, interested-in Mutual matching only Until you change it or delete your account Overwritten on edit; deleted with the account
8 Profile photos (approved) Profile and map face Until you delete the photo or your account Photo removed from storage and database when you delete it. On account deletion: database record and storage file deleted
9 Photos held or rejected by moderation Safety Held: until a human decides. Rejected: deleted at decision Database record deleted on rejection; storage file deleted within 30 days (proposed)
10 Moderation flags on photos (e.g. "nudity") Safety, DSA records While the photo exists Deleted with the photo
11 True location + last-seen time Now map and nearby matching Overwritten every ~15 min while the app is open; you stop appearing on the map 24 h after last update. Cleared automatically after 30 days without an update Overwritten; deleted with the account
12 Display offset (fixed per account) Location fuzzing Until account deletion Deleted with the account
13 Plans (place, times, vibes, interested-in) Later matching Until cancelled or account deletion. Deleted automatically 30 days after the plan's end Cancelled plans hidden immediately; deleted with the account
14 1:1 messages Conversations Until account deletion of either participant. "Delete" in a chat hides a message for you only Deleted when the sender's account is deleted, or when the conversation is deleted because either participant deleted their account
15 Group chats, members, group messages Conversations Until the group's creator deletes their account (the group is then deleted), or you leave/delete your account Cascade deletion
16 Group invites Inviting into groups Until account deletion. Pending/declined invites deleted automatically after 90 days Cascade deletion
17 Read markers (unread badges) Unread counts Until account deletion Cascade deletion
18 AI activity suggestions (cached plans) Avoid repeat AI calls Reused for 12 h. Deleted automatically after 30 days, or when the chat is deleted Cascade deletion with the chat
19 Chat text sent to Anthropic for suggestions Generate plans Not stored by us beyond the request; Anthropic retains API inputs/outputs per its commercial terms ([CONFIRM — e.g. up to 30 days, no training]) Per Anthropic DPA
20 Blocks Safety Until account deletion of either user Cascade deletion
21 Reports (reason, linked message, status, decision note, timestamps) DSA notice-and-action, safety 2 years after the decision; longer if needed for legal claims or authorities. Kept (with the account link removed) if either person deletes their account Deleted automatically by a daily job
22 Suspension status and statement of reasons DSA, preventing ban evasion 2 years after termination (proposed) Manual / scheduled purge (to build)
23 Appeals correspondence (email) DSA Art. 20 2 years after the decision (proposed) Delete from mailbox
24 Push token Message notifications Until you sign out / disable notifications / delete account Overwritten or deleted with the account
25 Server logs (IP, request metadata, errors) Security, debugging Per hosting provider defaults: [SUPABASE LOG RETENTION, e.g. 1–7 days on current plan] Automatic expiry
26 Image cache (on your phone) Faster loading Until the OS clears it or you delete the app Device-managed
27 Privacy/data-rights requests and responses Accountability 3 years (proposed) Delete from mailbox
28 Payment records (only if an onboarding fee is introduced) Tax/accounting 7 years (Dutch fiscal retention obligation) Per payment provider + accounting records
