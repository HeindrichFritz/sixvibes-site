Delete your Six Vibes account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Six Vibes and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap My settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, photos, plans, chats and messages. It cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email info@sixvibes.org from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

  • Reports you made or that were made about you are kept for up to 2 years with your identity removed, because EU law (the Digital Services Act) requires us to keep a record of moderation decisions.
  • Backups held by our hosting provider roll over within their normal backup cycle.

Everything else is deleted. See the Data Retention Schedule and Privacy Policy for details.
