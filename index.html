SixVibes

Available and bored? Find someone likeminded nearby.

Six Vibes is a mobile app for adults. Say what you're up for and when, and see who nearby is up for the same thing — right now, or later in a city you're travelling to.

activechillculturenightlifeexploreromance

Now — a map of faces near you who are available today.

Later — make a plan for a place and time ("Prague, Thursday 17:00–22:00") and see who'll be there too.

Chats — text only, 200 characters per message, with Report and Block on everything.

Six Vibes is 18+ only and every account passes an age check before it can do anything. Your exact location is never shown to anyone.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Questions? Write to info@sixvibes.org.
