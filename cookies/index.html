Six Vibes — Cookie & Device Storage Notice

Draft — this document is under legal review and may change before launch.

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE — confirm on publication; planned launch 13 November 2026]

Six Vibes is a mobile app. Our website, https://www.sixvibes.org, only has information pages — there is nothing to log into there — and neither the website nor the app uses advertising cookies, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers or analytics SDKs. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Like any app, Six Vibes stores a few things on your phone so it works. Under EU ePrivacy rules (in the Netherlands, Art. 11.7a Telecommunicatiewet) storage that is strictly necessary for a service you asked for does not need consent. Everything below is strictly necessary for the app to work. Mapbox's optional telemetry is switched off in the app.

What is stored on your device Why How long
Sign-in session (an access token and refresh token, in the app's private storage) Keeps you signed in so you don't have to sign in every time you open the app Until you sign out, delete your account or delete the app
Image cache (copies of profile photos you have viewed) Photos load quickly and don't flicker or re-download Until your phone clears its cache or you delete the app
App update files Receiving bug fixes and improvements to the app Replaced by each new update
Notification permission and push token (managed by your phone's operating system) Telling you when you receive a message — only if you allowed notifications Until you turn off notifications or delete the app
Location permission (managed by your phone's operating system) Your choice whether the app may use your location while open Until you change it in your phone's settings

Third-party services that run inside the app

  • Mapbox displays the map and powers place search when you create a plan. Its SDK caches map tiles on your phone so the map loads quickly. Mapbox's optional telemetry (usage statistics) is switched off in Six Vibes.

Public web pages

Our website https://www.sixvibes.org (including these legal documents) is a set of plain pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. GitHub, as the host, receives your IP address to deliver the pages. If we ever add non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this notice.

Your choices

  • Sign out (Profile → Settings) to remove your session from the device.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Change location and notification permissions at any time in your phone's settings.
