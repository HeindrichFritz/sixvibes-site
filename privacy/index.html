This policy explains what personal data the Six Vibes app ("Six Vibes", "we", "us") collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. Six Vibes is a mobile app for adults (18+) who want to find people nearby who are up for the same thing — anything from a coffee or a hike to a night out or a date.

We have written this in plain English. If anything is unclear, contact us at info@sixvibes.org.

2. The short version

  • Other users never see your exact location. They see a deliberately shifted and rounded position, which can be up to 24 hours old, and never a distance to you. Incognito hides you from the map immediately.
  • Gender and interested-in are optional and never shown on your profile. They are used only for mutual matching, with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw in Settings without deleting your account.
  • Everyone passes an 18+ age check (by our provider Didit) before they can use the app. We keep only the yes/no result and when it happened — never your selfie or ID.
  • No ads, no ad tracking, no analytics SDKs, no selling of data.
  • Push notifications are only ever sent for messages you receive, and they don't show the message text.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Profile → Settings → Delete account).

4. How location works (please read)

Location is the most sensitive thing this app handles, so here is exactly what happens:

  1. When it's collected. Only while the app is open on your screen, and only if you have allowed location access. It is refreshed when you open the app and about every 15 minutes while it stays open. We never collect location in the background.
  2. Where it's kept. Your true position is stored only on our server and is never sent to another user's phone. Which faces appear in a map view is worked out from the shifted and rounded positions (not true ones), and very small map views are widened, so moving or zooming the map can't be used to narrow down where someone really is.
  3. What others see. On the "Now" map, other people see your photo at a position that is (a) shifted by a fixed random offset of up to a few hundred metres that is chosen once for your account and never changes, and then (b) rounded to a grid cell of roughly 300–500 metres. People in the same cell are shown as one stacked group. Nobody is ever shown a distance to you.
  4. How long you appear. You appear on the Now map only if the app has seen your location in the last 24 hours. Turn on Incognito (Profile → Account) to disappear from the map immediately.
  5. Plans are different. A "Later" plan shows the place you chose (e.g. a city, neighbourhood or venue) to people whose plans match yours. If you move the plan's pin by hand, others see that pin. Don't put a plan's pin on your home.
  6. Place search. When you type a place name for a plan, your search text and your phone's IP address are sent to Mapbox to find matching places.
  7. Activity ideas. Only once everyone in a chat has agreed (see §7), "Suggest Activities" works out a rough midpoint of the participants' positions on our server, rounds it to about 1 km, and sends that rounded point to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (or, as a back-up, komoot's Photon) to get a neighbourhood or city name. Only that name — never coordinates — is sent to the AI provider.

5. Gender and "interested in" — sensitive data

What you choose here can reveal information about your sex life or sexual orientation, which is "special category" data under the GDPR. Giving it is optional: you can skip it when you sign up. If you skip it or withdraw consent, you can still use the rest of the app, but you won't appear on the map or in plans and won't see others there, because matching needs it. We:

  • ask for separate, affirmative explicit consent (a checkbox) before using these preferences, and record when you gave it;
  • let you withdraw consent at any time in Profile → Settings → Gender & interested in → Withdraw consent and remove these. This deletes both settings straight away; your account stays;
  • never show them on your profile;
  • use them only to decide who can see whom, and only where both sides match each other's choices;
  • never use them for analytics, never share them with any third party, never send them to the AI service, and never log them.

Please be aware that because matching is mutual, the fact that someone can see you implies that your choices include their gender. We consider this inherent to the service.

Your vibes (including "romance") are shown on your profile. Choose the ones you're comfortable with others seeing.

6. Age verification

Six Vibes is strictly 18+. Before you can browse, message, appear on the map or upload anything, you complete an age check with our provider Didit on Didit's own page: a selfie with a liveness check and an automatic age estimate. If the estimate is under 25, Didit also asks for an ID document (the minimum age of 18 is applied per country); clearly under-age estimates are declined. Our server then asks Didit for the result directly — the app can't mark itself verified. We store only a yes/no result and its time. We never receive or keep your selfie, ID document or name. 8. Automated decisions

  • Photo moderation: an automated system checks every photo. Clean photos are approved automatically; anything flagged is held for a human to review. A photo is never rejected solely by the machine.
  • Age estimation: an automated system estimates whether you are 18+. If it fails you, you can contact info@sixvibes.org to request a human review.
  • Matching: who you see is decided automatically from location, vibes, gender/interested-in and times. This has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

9. How long we keep data

In short: most data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete it. Some safety records (reports, moderation decisions) are kept longer to meet legal obligations. See our Data Retention Schedule for the full table.

Deleting a message in a chat hides it for you only; the other person still has their copy until they or you delete your accounts.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy (portability);
  • correct it (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • delete it — use Delete account in the app, or email us;
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing without needing to delete your account — for gender/interested-in in Profile → Settings, and for activity ideas in each chat's Suggest Activities sheet;
  • not be subject to purely automated decisions with significant effects (see §8).

To exercise a right, email info@sixvibes.org from the email address on your account. We will reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority — in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), or the authority in the EU country where you live.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Sensitive account fields (such as age-verified and suspended) can only be changed by our server, never by the app itself. Access is controlled by database-level rules so users can only read what they're meant to see. Server-only keys never ship inside the app. Only the single operator has administrative access. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will notify you and the authority as the law requires.

12. Children

Six Vibes is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that a user is under 18, the account is suspended immediately and removed. Report a suspected minor in the app or at info@sixvibes.org.

13. Changes

If we make significant changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect. The effective date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
